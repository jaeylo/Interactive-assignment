Light Dungeon / Mirror Puzzle

Aim your character's flashlight, move the mirror, and reflect the beam onto the exit sensor.
FLASHLIGHT → MIRROR → SENSOR → EXIT

CONTROLS

WASD / ↑↓←→ Move character
Mouse Aim flashlight
Drag mirror Move mirror
Q / E Rotate mirror
R Reset room

OBJECTIVE

Aim the flashlight at the mirror, then adjust the mirror until the reflected beam reaches the red sensor beside the exit.
There is no separate controllable lamp: the beam comes directly from the character's flashlight. Click and drag the mirror to reposition it, then use Q/E to change its angle.
